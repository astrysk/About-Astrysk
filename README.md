Hi, I am Astrysk.

I'm interested in problems that don't have obvious answers and in building solutions that work beyond a demo.

My work spans business, product, strategy, and technology. I look at problems from the ground up: what's actually going wrong, why existing approaches fall short, what a better solution looks like, and what it takes to build and deliver it.

This is where I put that work into the open.

You'll find software, AI applications, tools, experiments, and systems built to solve real problems. Not just the finished implementation, but the thinking, technical decisions, and business considerations behind it.

The goal is to build things that are useful, make them accessible, and give others something they can use, improve, or build upon.

WHAT I BUILD

Tools, software, AI applications, automations, and systems designed to solve practical problems.

My approach covers the entire process:

- Identifying problems worth solving.
- Researching the problem and evaluating existing solutions.
- Defining the solution and its requirements.
- Choosing the right technology and designing the system.
- Building, testing, and improving the implementation.
- Understanding the business model, distribution, and adoption.
- Documenting the results and making the work accessible.

I care about both the problem and the implementation. A solution needs to make sense in the real world, not just work in a demonstration.

AREAS OF INTEREST

- AI & Software: AI applications, intelligent systems, automation, APIs, and software development.
- Product & Systems: Problem discovery, product architecture, workflows, decision systems, and system design.
- Business & Strategy: Business models, operational problems, market opportunities, and strategic decision-making.
- Growth & Distribution: Product positioning, marketing, customer acquisition, sales, and go-to-market execution.

These areas overlap. Some problems require software. Others require a better process, a different business model, or a combination of approaches.

The objective is to find out what works.
